# Inorganic

Models of inorganic compounds: alkali halides, hydrogen chloride, nitrogen and oxygen.

The references of the force fields in this directory.

## AlkaliHalides-Walz2018a-LJ12_6

`AlkaliHalides-Walz2018a-LJ12_6.xml`: Alkali halides with Lennard-Jones 12-6.

- Marie-Madeleine Walz, Mohammad M. Ghahremanpour, Paul J. van Maaren and David van der Spoel, "Phase-Transferable Force Field for Alkali Halides", *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* **14**, pages 5933-5948 (2018). [doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00507](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00507)

## AlkaliHalides-Walz2018a-WBHAM

`AlkaliHalides-Walz2018a-WBHAM.xml`: Alkali halides with the Wang-Buckingham potential.

- Marie-Madeleine Walz, Mohammad M. Ghahremanpour, Paul J. van Maaren and David van der Spoel, "Phase-Transferable Force Field for Alkali Halides", *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* **14**, pages 5933-5948 (2018). [doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00507](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00507)

## HCl-Powles1978a

`HCl-Powles1978a.xml`: Hydrogen chloride.

- J. G. Powles, W. A. B. Evans, E. McGrath, K. E. Gubbins and S. Murad, "A computer simulation for a simple model of liquid hydrogen chloride", *Molecular Physics* **38**, pages 893-908 (1979). [doi:10.1080/00268977900102121](https://doi.org/10.1080/00268977900102121)

## N2-Cheung1975a

`N2-Cheung1975a.xml`: Nitrogen.

- P. S. Y. Cheung and J. G. Powles, "The properties of liquid nitrogen", *Molecular Physics* **30**, pages 921-949 (1975). [doi:10.1080/00268977500102461](https://doi.org/10.1080/00268977500102461)

## N2-Jordan1995a

`N2-Jordan1995a.xml`: Nitrogen, phase transferable.

- Peter C. Jordan, Paul J. van Maaren, Janez Mavri, David van der Spoel and Herman J. C. Berendsen, "Towards phase transferable potential functions: Methodology and application to nitrogen", *The Journal of Chemical Physics* **103**, pages 2272-2285 (1995). [doi:10.1063/1.469703](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.469703)

## O2-Cheung1976a

`O2-Cheung1976a.xml`: Oxygen.

- P. S. Y. Cheung, "On the efficient evaluation of torques and forces for anisotropic potentials in computer simulation of liquids composed of linear molecules", *Chemical Physics Letters* **40**, pages 19-22 (1976). [doi:10.1016/0009-2614(76)80110-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/0009-2614(76)80110-8)
