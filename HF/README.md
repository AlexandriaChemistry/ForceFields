# HF

Models of hydrogen fluoride.

The references of the force fields in this directory.

## Cournoyer1984a

`Cournoyer1984a.xml`: Three-site pair potential (TIPS type).

- Michael E. Cournoyer and William L. Jorgensen, "An improved intermolecular potential function for simulations of liquid hydrogen fluoride", *Molecular Physics* **51**, pages 119–132 (1984). [doi:10.1080/00268978400100081](https://doi.org/10.1080/00268978400100081)

## Jedlovszky1997a

`Jedlovszky1997a.xml`: Rigid three-site effective pair potential (JV-NP).

- Pál Jedlovszky and Renzo Vallauri, "Computer simulation study of liquid HF with a new effective pair potential model", *Molecular Physics* **92**, pages 331–336 (1997). [doi:10.1080/002689797170536](https://doi.org/10.1080/002689797170536)

## Jedlovszky1997b

`Jedlovszky1997b.xml`: Polarizable three-site model with a shell on fluorine (JV-P).

- Pál Jedlovszky and Renzo Vallauri, "Computer simulations of liquid HF by a newly developed polarizable potential model", *The Journal of Chemical Physics* **107**, pages 10166–10176 (1997). [doi:10.1063/1.474152](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.474152)

## Orabi2020a

`Orabi2020a.xml`: Three-site model for simulations in chemistry and biology.

- Esam A. Orabi and José D. Faraldo-Gómez, "New Molecular-Mechanics Model for Simulations of Hydrogen Fluoride in Chemistry and Biology", *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* **16**, pages 5105–5126 (2020). [doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.0c00247](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.0c00247)
